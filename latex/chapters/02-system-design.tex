\chapter{System Design}
\label{chap:system-design}

Describe the overall architecture and the interaction between sensors,
controller, power supply, and watering actuator.

\section{Hardware architecture}

Document the ESP32 development board, moisture sensor, pump or valve, driver
circuit, and power arrangement. The ESP32 is documented in the manufacturer's
datasheet~\cite{esp32-datasheet}.

\section{Software architecture}

Describe the tasks, timing, shared state, and error handling. The firmware uses
the Embassy asynchronous embedded framework~\cite{embassy-book}.

\section{Control strategy}

Explain how sensor measurements are converted into watering decisions,
including thresholds, hysteresis, time limits, and safety behaviour.
